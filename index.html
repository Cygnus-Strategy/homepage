Before You Ask

The questions that come up on most first calls.

Can one person really cover all six layers?
Not all at once, and not at equal depth. The six are the range built up over fifteen years of running them, and a given engagement usually lives in two or three. The range matters because problems rarely stay inside one layer, and you do not get handed off at the boundary when they move.
What happens when the engagement ends?
We build with your engineers rather than around them. Where we have implemented something, you keep the documentation and the people who can run it. The goal is that you stop needing us.
Will you sign an NDA?
Before any sensitive discussion. We have written and enforced SOC 2 and HIPAA programs at multiple companies and hold ourselves to the standard we recommend. The full information security policy is available on request.
